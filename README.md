PROJECT PROPOSAL:
Web Travel Planner -Ecofriendly  is a current interactive web application which is intended to assist the user in making ecological friendly trip plans in an easy and responsible manner. It uses React.js, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as a platform and a focus on a nature-oriented and clean responsive interface.This project aims at offering individual, couple, family and group travelers an easy means of exploring environmentally friendly destinations, designing their own customized itineraries and understanding the concept of eco-friendly traveling.
The project reflects the ability of front-end development and the skill of UI/UX design through the combination of intuitive design, reusability, and the use of natural colors.

The site has essential pages, including Home, Destinations, Plan Trip, and Eco Tips with their smooth navigation, attractive design, and informative design.
The project is a representation of more than just technical implementation but it also shows the determination of disseminating green travel awareness in a digital friendly manner.


UI-UX ANALYSIS:
The user survey on the Web Travel Planner project was a component of the first stage of the project because it involved surveying the frequent travelers and the environment-conscious people in order to familiarize themselves with their travel behaviors, priorities, and sustainability consciousness.

The obtained data showed that people were interested in such features as:

Filtering and searching destinations.

Customizable itineraries How to be an eco-friendly traveler.An easy to use, lightweight interface.
These insights helped us to design our user flow and wireframes which were created in Figma.
Every page, such as the hero banner, the trip planner, and so on, has a user-oriented design template that creates a smooth experience with user-friendly navigation, spacing, and nature-friendly imagery.

This research-based strategy makes the platform accessible and engaging with a consistent visual look with the decisions made in design-related areas which correspond with the actual expectations of users.

COMPETITOR ANALYSIS :

The most popular travel planning platforms include TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Traveloka, which also include destination discovery and booking capabilities, yet do not underline the issue of sustainability and environmental responsibility.
Although these sites place great emphasis on commercial bookings and adverts, Web Travel Planner -Ecofriendly Edition is different because:
Emphasizing green destinations.
Encouraging responsible traveling hints.
Voluntary itinerary planning.
Having a minimal, no distraction interface to user of any age.

We are not going to position our project as a competitor but as an eco-awareness tool, motivating travelers to make green choices and organize trips in a way that has a minimum impact on the environment.

This considerate strategy makes the user experience viable and inspirational to facilitate sustainability ambitions globally as well as streamlining the process of planning his or her trip.

CONCLUSION:
Web Travel Planner was designed on the basis of the vision to make travel enjoyable and responsible. The platform enables people to organize unforgettable journeys by utilizing the latest web technologies and being eco-friendly.

This project will demonstrate our interest in creating easy-to-use, meaningful digital solutions by its clear structure, interactive design, and educational eco-tips to promote positive global change.
In the end, it also shows how frontend development and a design-driven mind can help to create awareness about an environmentally more sustainable, interconnected world.
